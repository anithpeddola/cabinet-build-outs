# Cabinet Build-Outs

**Wipro, Hyderabad, India · Mar 2021 – Jan 2023**
**Role: Onsite Support**

Full cabinet builds to layout plans, from equipment receiving through structured cabling and handover.

## What the work involves
- Handle equipment receiving for servers, switches, and related hardware, racking and securing each unit following rack layout plans
- Complete cabinet build-outs to layout, positioning hardware inside cabinets and following safety procedures during installs
- Run structured cabling for network equipment, keeping cable routing, labeling, and organization consistent across installs
- Log deployments, hardware changes, and issue resolutions in the ticketing system, keeping ticket tracking current for shift handovers
- Maintain asset tracking records for spare parts inventory and deployed hardware

## Skills & tools
Cabinet build-outs · Rack layout plans · Equipment receiving · Structured cabling · Asset tracking · Spare parts inventory · Ticket tracking
